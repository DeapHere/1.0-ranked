Oldschool pace tracker (Minecraft 1.0)

Not a mod. It needs StatsPerWorld installed, and reads the stats file StatsPerWorld keeps in your newest
world (play time and items used), plus whether that world has its Nether and End folders yet
and when its region files were last saved. It never opens level.dat or any other world data, never shows you anything you don't already know, and
never touches the game. It sends your splits to the Oldschool Ranked server, which posts good paces to
the Discord.

1. Double-click oldschool-pace-tracker.jar (needs Java, which you already have for Minecraft).
2. Type your Minecraft name, then pick your 1.0 instance folder. Playing a wall? Ctrl-click to pick all of
   your instances at once. Every world is tracked on its own, so resetting never breaks the run you're
   actually playing. Change your instances, name or Twitch (linked in the Discord posts) from the gear.
3. Leave the little window open while you play. Start a new world and go.

Splits:
  Nether        you first go into the Nether
  Fortress      you first mine a fortress block: nether brick, nether brick fence or stairs, or nether wart
  Pit start     you place a pressure plate, then die (the death reset to spawn), outside the Nether
  End           you first go into the End
  Finish        you leave the End through the exit portal (not by dying)
Splits can show up a few seconds late, because the game only saves stats every few seconds.
A run is only tracked from a brand new world. Settings are in .oldschoolranked/pace.properties in your
user folder.
